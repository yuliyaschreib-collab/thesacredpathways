Coconut Ladoo

Soft, creamy coconut sweets prepared in the Thermomix TM6. This version uses Diabliss cane sugar and semi-skimmed milk powder. The original trial was successful; further texture and cardamom refinements are recorded below.

TM618 small ladoosAbout 25 minutes activeVegetarian · Contains dairy

Ingredients

    About Diabliss: Diabliss is cane-sugar-based, not sugar-free. This recipe contains naturally occurring milk sugars and added Diabliss sugar.

    Thermomix TM6 method

    1. Powder the sugar. Place Diabliss in a dry mixing bowl. Mix 10 sec / speed 10. Scrape down. Leave it in the bowl.
    2. Combine. Add milk, milk powder and ghee. Mix 20 sec / speed 4, then scrape down.
    3. Heat. Cook 5 min / 90°C / speed 2, measuring cup in place.
    4. Add coconut. Add desiccated coconut and cardamom. Mix 20 sec / reverse / speed 3.
    5. Cook. Cook 6 min / 90°C / reverse / speed 2, measuring cup removed and simmering basket placed on the lid. The mixture should be moist but not runny. If needed, continue in 2-minute increments.
    6. Shape. Cool for 10–15 minutes until safe to handle. Divide into equal portions, roll into balls and coat in the reserved coconut.
    TM6 capacity note: The serving selector scales ingredients only, not cooking times. For 36 ladoos, prepare two batches of 18 using the tested settings. For other yields, reassess cooking time and texture.

    Kitchen notes & next trial

    Tested result: The original recipe tasted good and held together successfully.

    Proposed texture improvement (not yet tested): Pulse the desiccated coconut briefly before adding it, beginning with 5 sec / speed 7. Stop before it becomes oily or turns into coconut butter.

    Proposed flavour improvement (not yet tested): Increase cardamom from ¼ tsp to ¾ tsp for the 18-piece batch, then adjust to taste.

    Storage

    Cool promptly and store in an airtight container in the refrigerator. As a cautious homemade-dairy guideline, use within 3–4 days. Keep chilled during transport and until serving.
